Bulk AI

Dynamic, adherence-neutral nutrition tracker. Forked from Fud AI (MIT) for personal use.

Add to AltStore

  1. Open AltStore on your iPhone.
  2. Browse tab → Sources → tap the +.
  3. Paste this URL:
https://edrickchang13.github.io/food/source.json

AltStore will fetch the manifest, show Bulk AI in the source, and offer the latest build to install. Each push to main kicks off a fresh build automatically.
